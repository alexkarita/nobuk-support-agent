# Nobuk Help Center

## What is Nobuk?
Nobuk is a payment collection and reconciliation platform for African
businesses and communities. It automates reconciliation and generates
instant reports so there is no guesswork.

## Payment Links
Create branded payment links in seconds with customizable options. Share
them through email, messaging apps, or social media.

## Payment Channels
You can collect through Paybill, Buy Goods/Till number, or Bank Account.
Nobuk does not hold your funds — all collections go directly to the
payment channel you have linked.

## Collection Pages
Set up your custom collection page in minutes. No technical skills
required.

## Subscriptions
Nobuk supports subscription-based payments with automatic reminders.

## Reporting
Nobuk tracks all payments in real time, provides comprehensive analytics,
auto-reconciliation, and instant branded reports.

## Getting Started
1. Create an account on nobuk.africa
2. Link your Paybill, Till number, or bank account
3. Create a payment link or collection page
4. Share it with your community and monitor payments in real time

## Pricing & Support
For pricing, contact Nobuk through the website. For technical issues,
file a support ticket and a human agent will follow up.
